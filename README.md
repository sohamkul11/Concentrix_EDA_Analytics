# Concentrix ANZ – Data Analyst Case Study

This project presents an end-to-end **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Root Cause Analysis (RCA)** of operational performance for Concentrix ANZ. The analysis combines three datasets — **Agent Master, Weekly Performance, and Weekly Targets** — to investigate the decline in Quality Assurance (QA) performance and understand its relationship with key operational and customer-experience metrics. After data cleaning, validation, and merging, the final dataset contained **5,200 records covering 200 agents across 26 weeks**. Using **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly**, the analysis examines QA performance against targets across weeks, teams, shifts, agent tenure, and issue specialisations, while also analysing **Average Handling Time (AHT), First Contact Resolution (FCR), Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), and contacts handled per agent**. The analysis identified a significant deterioration in performance from **Week 15 onwards**, with average QA declining from **81.03 to 75.88**, AHT increasing from **6.51 to 7.33 minutes**, FCR decreasing from **76.64% to 73.66%**, and CSAT declining from **3.35 to 3.22**, while average contact volume remained almost unchanged. Further analysis highlighted higher-risk segments, including **Team Beta, Team Delta, agents with 6–12 months of tenure, and the Evening shift**. Correlation analysis also identified strong relationships between QA and other operational KPIs, particularly FCR, AHT, and CSAT; however, these relationships are interpreted as associations rather than proof of causation. Based on the findings, the project recommends **targeted coaching, investigation of process and handling inefficiencies, improvements in First Contact Resolution, shift-level operational reviews, and continuous monitoring of QA, AHT, FCR, and CSAT**. The project demonstrates how data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualisation, segmentation, correlation analysis, and root-cause investigation can be combined to generate actionable, evidence-based business insights.
